What will be in my box?

A seasonal selection of the fruit and vegetables available from our growers. Variety counts describe the box range; the exact produce is confirmed when you enquire.

Can I choose how often it arrives?

You can request weekly or fortnightly delivery in your basket. We’ll confirm the schedule and availability with you before an order is arranged.

How do I place an order?

Add your favourites to the basket and choose “Prepare order email”. This opens a draft in your email app. Send it to ask about availability, delivery, and the final total. No payment or subscription happens on this website.

What if I have allergies or exclusions?

Please include any requirements in your order enquiry. Wait for confirmation from the farm before relying on a box meeting your needs.
