Before the first box

A few useful
little details.

Do you deliver to my area?

Delivery coverage is confirmed directly with the farm. Include your Portuguese postcode in your enquiry so we can check before arranging an order.

Am I signing up to a subscription?

No. Adding to your basket or sending an enquiry does not create a subscription. Your requested delivery rhythm is a preference to discuss with the farm.

Can I talk to someone first?

Of course. Use our contact form to prepare a message, or email hello@localfarm.com directly.
