Local Farm began with a simple idea: make it easier to bring fresh fruit and vegetables from local growers to the people who enjoy them.

Along the way, we want to change the way we look at food. A small apple or a curvy courgette can still be the beginning of a very good meal.

Our aim is to connect communities, make space for imperfect produce, and help every harvest go a little further.
